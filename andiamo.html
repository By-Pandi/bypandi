04 — Bachelor Thesis

Andiamo

TFG Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya Barcelona 2023

Human mobility, whether on foot or by vehicle, is essential for experiencing life. Technologies like automobiles have evolved to meet this need but have led to congested streets. Now, alternatives such as the skateboard are being explored as ideal solutions for urban transport. In industrial design, efforts focus on sustainable solutions that enhance people's lives. This project aims to explore different mechanisms that make the skateboard more transportable, positioning it as a viable form of micromobility that facilitates everyday urban mobility.
